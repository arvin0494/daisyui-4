### Diff
Diff component shows a side-by-side comparison of two items.

[Diff documentation](https://v4.daisyui.com/components/diff/)

#### Class names
- component: `diff`, `diff-item-1`, `diff-item-2`, `diff-resizer`

#### Syntax
```html
<div class="diff">Content</div>
```
#### Rules
- `diff-item-1` and `diff-item-2` are the two compared items; `diff-resizer` makes it draggable.
