### Avatar
Avatars are used to show a thumbnail representation of an individual or business in the interface.

[Avatar documentation](https://v4.daisyui.com/components/avatar/)

#### Class names
- component: `avatar`, `avatar-group`
- modifier: `online`, `offline`, `placeholder`

#### Syntax
```html
<div class="avatar-group -space-x-6">
  <div class="avatar"><div class="w-12"></div></div>
  <div class="avatar"><div class="w-12"></div></div>
</div>
```
#### Rules
- `avatar-group` stacks avatars with overlap. Add `online`, `offline`, or `placeholder` to an avatar div.
