### Collapse
Collapse is used for showing and hiding content.

[Collapse documentation](https://v4.daisyui.com/components/collapse/)

#### Class names
- component: `collapse`, `collapse-title`, `collapse-content`
- behavior: `collapse-open`, `collapse-close`
- modifier: `collapse-arrow`, `collapse-plus`

#### Syntax
```html
<div class="collapse">
  <div class="collapse-title">Title</div>
  <div class="collapse-content">Content</div>
</div>
```
#### Rules
- `collapse-title` and `collapse-content` are the clickable header and body.
- Add `collapse-arrow` or `collapse-plus` for an icon; `collapse-open` / `collapse-close` force a state.
