### Indicator
Indicators are used to place an element on the corner of another element.

[Indicator documentation](https://v4.daisyui.com/components/indicator/)

#### Class names
- component: `indicator`, `indicator-item`
- modifier: `indicator-start`, `indicator-center`, `indicator-end`, `indicator-top`, `indicator-middle`, `indicator-bottom`

#### Syntax
```html
<div class="indicator">
  <div class="indicator-item">New</div>
  <div>Item</div>
</div>
```
#### Rules
- `indicator-item` positions a badge on the corner of a parent that has the `indicator` class.
